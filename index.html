4. Health answers

This section applies to Lever only. Lever Coach does not ask about health.

What. Answers about injuries and medical conditions: joint pain, a heart condition, pregnancy; and any pain you report after a workout. Under the GDPR these are "special category" data (Article 9).

Why. Only to adjust your program: swap exercises and lower loads. Nothing else.

How we ask. Lever first shows a one-time notice that it is not a medical service. It then asks an optional question: may your injury and medical answers and any pain you report after a workout be used to adjust your program? If you say no, those answers are deleted and the program is built without them. If you say yes, they are saved. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) GDPR).

Where they are kept. On your phone, in the app's private storage. They go to our cloud database (Supabase, Ireland) only if you are also signed in. In Lever for Android, backup of the app's data is turned off, so the answers are not in your Google backup. On iPhone, if you turned on iCloud backup, the app's data, including the answers, is in that backup; it is kept in your Apple account and we cannot read it.

Who never gets them. Your coach, Amplitude, Google Firebase, RevenueCat, Google and anyone else. Two exceptions: Supabase stores them for us when you are signed in, and Apple holds your iCloud backup if you turned it on. They are never part of usage statistics or crash reports, and never used for advertising or any other purpose.

Withdrawing consent. Turn the health consent off in Lever's settings at any time. From then on Lever stops using the answers. Answers already saved are deleted when you delete your account, or within 30 days of your request to this.is.daily.staff@gmail.com. Without an account, clearing the app's data deletes them.

How long. Until you delete your account or ask us to delete them. Without an account: until you clear the app's data.

If you live in the United States, our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy also applies.

5. How Lever builds your program

Lever's program engine is a set of rules written by people: linear progression, planned deload weeks and staged progressions between exercise variants. It uses your quiz answers, your logged sets and, only if you consented, your health answers.

There is no machine-learning model and no generative AI inside Lever. Your data is not sent to any AI service and is not used to train any model. The engine only suggests exercises and weights; you can change every suggestion. It makes no decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you (Article 22 GDPR).

In Lever Coach, workouts and programs are written by the coach.

6. Usage statistics and crash reports

Which apps. Lever (Android and iOS) and Lever Coach (Android and iOS) send usage statistics to Amplitude, in the versions published in the stores. Lever for Android also uses Google Firebase Analytics for usage statistics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports. Lever for iOS and Lever Coach have no crash-reporting library.

Why. To see which screens and features are used, where people stop or run into errors, how many people start and keep a subscription, and how long workouts are, so that we can fix and improve the apps. Never for advertising; never shared with advertisers or data brokers; never combined with data from other companies.

Legal basis. Our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): understanding which screens and features are used, finding errors and crashes, and improving the apps. There is no switch for this in the apps. You can object at any time (Article 21 GDPR): write to this.is.daily.staff@gmail.com, and we will ask Amplitude and Google to delete everything tied to your device ID. Deleting the app also stops the collection.

What goes to Amplitude:

  • usage events: which screens you open and which features you use, with counts and types of actions;
  • a device ID. On iPhone this is Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV): it is the same in Lever and Lever Coach on one iPhone, and it is not an advertising identifier. On Android, Amplitude creates a random ID when the app is installed. Neither is your account ID; we do not send Amplitude your account ID;
  • your IP address. Amplitude uses it to work out your country and city, and stores it with the events; your mobile carrier and the device manufacturer;
  • device model, operating system version, app version, app language;
  • purchase events: product ID, plan and currency; in Lever also the price you paid, in Lever Coach without amounts;
  • training counts: the number of sets and the length of a session, without weights.

What goes to Firebase (Lever for Android): the same kind of usage events with device and app information and a Firebase app-instance ID; for a crash, what the app was doing when it crashed, the device model, operating system version, app version and a Firebase installation ID. Firebase does not receive the advertising ID of your Android device: its collection is turned off in the app.

What never goes to any of them: your name, e-mail address, account ID, weights, body measurements, health answers, free text (notes, client names), and any data of a coach's clients.

Statistics are not linked to your account: we never send your account ID, name or e-mail. They are still personal data, because they are tied to a device ID and an IP address.

Deleting it. Because statistics are not linked to your account, deleting your account does not delete them. To erase them, write to us with your device ID, if you know it; otherwise tell us the app, your phone model, the app language and roughly when you installed the app, and we will look for the matching records and ask Amplitude and Google to delete them.

How long. Amplitude: until we delete them; Amplitude does not delete them automatically. Firebase Analytics: 2 months. Firebase Crashlytics: 90 days.

Apple diagnostics. If you chose to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple gives us aggregated crash and usage statistics under Apple's own policy.

9. What a coach sees, and what a client sees

9.1 A coach sees about a linked client

Only after the client taps "Share with coach" on a separate screen that lists this:

  • the client's name (if the account has no name, the part of the e-mail before the @; not for Apple private relay addresses);
  • workouts and program;
  • every logged workout: date, length, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, and records;
  • bookings, attendance and attendance answers, the sessions-left count;
  • when the coach runs a workout together with the client, the sets both of them enter, in real time.

A coach does not see the client's e-mail address (except a manual client's e-mail the coach typed in), body weight, height, age, goal, health and injury answers, pain reports, streaks, or anything about other coaches.

9.2 A client sees about the coach

  • the coach's name, open time, the client's own bookings and assigned workouts, the sessions-left count, and reminders;
  • never the coach's prices, private notes or other clients.

9.3 Stopping

A client can unlink at any time (Lever: You tab, coach card). A coach can end a link too. It takes effect at once on our server: the coach can no longer read any training data of that client. Future sessions between the two are cancelled and not deducted from the sessions-left count. The coach's workouts and assignments are removed from the client's Lever; the client keeps their own history. Linking again needs a new agreement on the consent screen.

What stays in the coach's account after unlinking is the coach's own record of sessions with that client: dates, times, status, the sessions-left count and its change history, the coach's notes and prices. The coach does not keep access to the client's training data.

If the person later signs in to Lever with a verified e-mail that matches, Lever offers the link on the usual consent screen. Nothing is shared until the person agrees, and then the earlier sessions and the sessions-left count are merged into their account.

If a coach added you. A coach can add you by typing your name and e-mail address. For this, and for the notes, prices and session counts the coach keeps about you, the coach is responsible (controller), and we store it only on the coach's instructions (processor, Article 28 GDPR). The coach got this information from you and must tell you about it. We use your e-mail address for one purpose of our own: if you sign in to Lever with that verified address, we offer you the link on the consent screen (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f): letting you accept or refuse the coach's invitation). We send you nothing before that. The record stays until the coach removes you or deletes their account. To see, correct or delete it, ask the coach, or write to us at this.is.daily.staff@gmail.com; we will pass the request to the coach and help carry it out.

11. Deleting your account and your data

We keep account data while the account exists (exact periods in section 7). Without an account, data lives only on your phone.

Delete in the app. Lever: Settings, Delete account. Lever Coach: Profile, Delete account. There are two confirmation screens: what will be deleted, then typing a confirmation word. You need an internet connection. Deletion is permanent and we cannot undo it.

Delete without the app. Send an e-mail to this.is.daily.staff@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Lever account" or "Delete my Lever Coach account". You do not need to install the app again. If you write from another address, we will ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted: your profile and sign-in record, workouts, programs, logs and sets, health answers, settings, devices and push tokens, notification records, bookings and sessions-left records with coaches or clients, and the link itself. For a coach also: the schedule, invite codes, session prices, manual-client records and our subscription record.

Effect on the other person. If a client deletes their account, the coach loses that client's training data, bookings and sessions-left records. If a coach deletes their account, clients keep their own training history, while the coach's workouts, assignments and bookings disappear from their app.

Sign in with Apple. When you delete an account created with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke the connection with this app.

What is not deleted by us:

  • purchase and tax records that Apple and Google keep under their own policies and the law. Your customer record at RevenueCat: write to us and we will delete it;
  • usage statistics and crash reports, because they are not linked to your account. How to delete them: section 6;
  • server logs, after the period in section 7. We do not keep database backups.

9. Що бачить тренер і що бачить клієнт

9.1 Що тренер бачить про пов’язаного клієнта

Лише після того, як клієнт натисне «Поділитися з тренером» на окремому екрані, де перелічено таке:

  • ім’я клієнта (якщо в обліковому записі немає імені — частину адреси електронної пошти до @; для прихованих адрес Apple — ні);
  • тренування і програму;
  • кожне записане тренування: дату, тривалість, вправи, підходи, повторення, ваги, час відпочинку і рекорди;
  • бронювання, відвідування і відповіді про відвідування, лічильник «залишилось занять»;
  • коли тренер проводить тренування разом із клієнтом — підходи, які вводять обоє, у реальному часі.

Тренер не бачить адреси електронної пошти клієнта (крім адреси клієнта, доданого вручну, яку ввів сам тренер), ваги тіла, зросту, віку, мети, відповідей про здоров’я і травми, повідомлень про біль, серій і нічого про інших тренерів.

9.2 Що клієнт бачить про тренера

  • ім’я тренера, вільний час, власні бронювання клієнта і призначені тренування, лічильник «залишилось занять» і нагадування;
  • ніколи — ціни тренера, особисті нотатки чи інших клієнтів.

9.3 Як припинити

Клієнт може розірвати зв’язок будь-коли (Lever: вкладка «Ви», картка тренера). Тренер теж може завершити зв’язок. Це діє одразу на нашому сервері: тренер більше не може читати жодних тренувальних даних цього клієнта. Майбутні заняття між ними скасовуються і не списуються з лічильника «залишилось занять». Тренування і призначення тренера зникають із Lever клієнта; клієнт зберігає власну історію. Щоб знову пов’язатися, потрібна нова згода на екрані згоди.

Після розірвання зв’язку в обліковому записі тренера залишаються його власні записи про заняття з цим клієнтом: дати, час, статус, лічильник «залишилось занять» та історія його змін, нотатки й ціни тренера. Доступу до тренувальних даних клієнта тренер не зберігає.
